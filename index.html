Budzeez Scheduler brings content planning, scheduled publishing and performance summaries into one privately operated workspace for Wandering Mind and Budzeez Finds.

Built for the channels we manage

The Windows-based application currently supports Facebook Page publishing and analytics. YouTube upload and analytics integration is being configured. It is not yet available as a public service.

Plan, publish, learn

Prepare captions and media, queue approved posts, and review available performance metrics. Saved reports help the operator compare content and plan future work.

Account access stays with the operator

Only accounts the operator is authorized to manage are connected. Google authorization, when enabled, is used to upload selected videos, read channel and video information, and retrieve YouTube Analytics reports. It does not require your Google password to be entered into this website.

This public site contains information only. It has no scheduler login, account connection form, or access to the private publishing database.
