Privacy policy

This policy covers Budzeez Scheduler, operated by Elliott White, and this public information website. The scheduler is a privately operated tool for accounts the operator owns or is authorized to manage. YouTube integration is in setup; the Google-data provisions below apply when that connection is enabled.

Information accessed

The scheduler stores post captions, selected media, publishing schedules, publication identifiers, status and error information, and available performance reports. Connected Facebook Page data includes Page identity, published posts and engagement metrics.

When authorized for YouTube, the application requests youtube.upload, youtube.readonly and yt-analytics.readonly access to upload selected videos, identify connected channels and videos, and retrieve performance reports. OAuth access and refresh tokens allow authorized requests without collecting Google passwords.

How information is used

Data is used to perform publishing actions chosen by the operator, display account and queue status, summarize performance, and diagnose connection or publishing failures. Google user data is not sold, used for advertising, or used to train generalized AI or machine-learning models.

Storage and access

Scheduler content and reports are stored on the operator’s Windows server and its backups. Existing Facebook credentials are encrypted for the Windows account. This information website does not store connected-account credentials or the publishing database. YouTube token storage is not yet active; its implementation will be reviewed before connection is enabled.

Sharing

Publishing sends the chosen content and associated metadata to the selected platform. Authorized API requests send the credentials needed by that platform. The operator may deliberately export reports and share them with an analysis service, such as ChatGPT; exports must exclude account tokens. There is no automatic transfer of the private database to this public site.

Retention and deletion

Local posts and analytics remain until removed by the operator. Backups can retain earlier copies until the operator removes those backups. To request deletion of stored account data, contact the address below. Revoking authorization stops future authorized access but does not itself delete local reports, backups, or posts already published on a platform.

You can revoke Google access through Google Account connections. You can manage or delete published videos in YouTube Studio. Facebook permissions can be removed through Facebook’s integration settings.

Public website

This site adds no advertising trackers, analytics scripts, or contact forms. The hosting provider may process technical request information, such as IP addresses and browser information, to deliver and protect the site. Contacting support by email shares your email address and message with the operator and email provider.

Google and YouTube

Budzeez Scheduler’s use and transfer of information received from Google APIs will adhere to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including its Limited Use requirements. See also Google’s Privacy Policy.

Questions and changes

For privacy or deletion requests, email bizzze96@gmail.com. Material changes to data handling will be reflected here with an updated effective date.
