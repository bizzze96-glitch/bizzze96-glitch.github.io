Privacy policy

This policy covers Budzeez Scheduler, operated by Elliott White, and this public information website. The scheduler is a privately operated tool for accounts the operator owns or is authorized to manage. Pinterest support is being added and will access Pinterest information only if the operator authorizes the connection.

Information accessed

The scheduler stores post captions, selected media, publishing schedules, publication identifiers, status and error information, and available performance reports. Connected Facebook Page data includes Page identity, published posts and engagement metrics.

For YouTube, the application requests youtube.upload, youtube.readonly and yt-analytics.readonly access to upload selected videos, identify connected channels and videos, and retrieve performance reports. When Pinterest is connected, the scheduler will request the permissions needed to read the operator's account and boards, create or manage Pins, and retrieve Pin analytics. These may include Pinterest account or profile information, board names and IDs, Pin images or videos, descriptions, links, publication IDs, and available performance metrics. OAuth access and refresh tokens allow authorized requests without collecting platform passwords.

How information is used

Data is used to perform publishing actions chosen by the operator, display account and queue status, summarize performance, and diagnose connection or publishing failures. Platform account data obtained through APIs is not sold, rented, used to target ads, or used to train generalized AI or machine-learning models. Affiliate links and required disclosures may be included in Pins that the operator chooses to publish.

Storage and access

Scheduler content, reports, connection tokens, and publishing records are stored on the operator’s Windows server and its backups. Connected-account credentials are used for authorized platform API requests. Google Drive may temporarily hold completed media and related publishing manifests for delivery to the home scheduler. This public information website does not store account credentials or the private publishing database.

Sharing

Publishing sends the chosen content and associated metadata to the selected platform. Authorized API requests send the credentials needed by that platform. The operator may deliberately export reports and share them with an analysis service, such as ChatGPT; exports must exclude account tokens. There is no automatic transfer of the private database to this public site.

Retention and deletion

Local posts and analytics remain until removed by the operator. Backups can retain earlier copies until the operator removes those backups. To request deletion of stored account data, contact the address below. Revoking authorization stops future authorized access but does not itself delete local reports, backups, or posts already published on a platform.

You can revoke Google access through Google Account connections. You can manage or delete published videos in YouTube Studio. Facebook permissions can be removed through Facebook’s integration settings. Pinterest access can be revoked through Pinterest's connected-app settings, and scheduled unpublished Pins can be canceled in the scheduler. Revoking access does not remove Pins already published to Pinterest; those can be managed in the Pinterest account.

Public website

This site adds no advertising trackers, analytics scripts, or contact forms. The hosting provider may process technical request information, such as IP addresses and browser information, to deliver and protect the site. Contacting support by email shares your email address and message with the operator and email provider.

Pinterest

Pinterest integration is intended to manage only the Pinterest business account and boards explicitly authorized by the operator. Requested permissions will be used to create and schedule selected Pins, assign them to boards, check publication results, prevent duplicate publishing, and review available analytics. No Pinterest account data is sold or shared with advertisers. A connected Pinterest account can be disconnected by revoking the app's access in Pinterest account settings; the operator may also request deletion of stored integration data using the contact address below.

Google and YouTube

Budzeez Scheduler’s use and transfer of information received from Google APIs will adhere to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including its Limited Use requirements. See also Google’s Privacy Policy.

Questions and changes
